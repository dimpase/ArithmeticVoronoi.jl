Two-cell and arithmetic group presentation results, 2026-10-17
==============================================================

Completed: exact two-cells, GL presentations, central scalar quotient,
index-four SL extraction, and Tietze simplification for D5 and D8.
All groups are K-linear on the maximal-order lattice O^2. Both retain -I.
GL includes the full scalar-unit group. No semilinear/projective quotient.

Field      ridges    local orbits   loops    GL raw / small     SL raw / small
D5      [75, 15]   [11, 4]      180    20/390 -> 3/10    77/1564 -> 2/6
D8      [15, 76]   [4, 11]      182    17/291 -> 3/11    65/1168 -> 2/6

Presentation sizes are generator count / relator count.
Global unoriented edge orbits: four in each field.
Unresolved coverage entries: zero in each field.
Global two-cell orbit minimization was not performed. The exhaustive local
ridge loops are deliberately redundant and cover every required orbit.
D8 has one inverted edge: reversing matrices have square I and order two;
its full setwise stabilizer has order four (the Klein four group).
D5 has no edge inversions.
