ArithmeticVoronoi uses PerfectForms.jl for perfect-form enumeration:
https://github.com/dimpase/PerfectForms.jl

PerfectForms owns the exporter originally developed here, the CMake build,
upstream revision pins and JSON process interface. It fetches Mathieu Dutour
Sikiric's polyhedral_common as an external build dependency and requires an
installed nauty/Traces library. Its backend/NOTICE.txt retains upstream
attribution and reference license texts. ArithmeticVoronoi contains no C++
source, upstream source checkout or backend executable in its tracked files.

Our Julia arithmetic models, exact validation, two-cells, presentations and
constructive words remain in ArithmeticVoronoi under the existing LICENSE.
OSCAR supplies the exact arithmetic and GAP interface; GAP performs
presentation simplification, independently of the PerfectForms exporter.

BACKEND_AUDIT.md and upstream_reports/ preserve the earlier backend audit
and issue submissions. The frozen mathematical archives retain the exporter
and build recipes used for those computations as historical provenance;
they include no upstream source tree or compiled executable. These immutable
archives were not regenerated during the package extraction.
